Habit Tracker App for 2027 — streaks, year heat map, works offline

This habit tracker app is for anyone who wants to check off a few habits each day and see the year at a glance. Set up your 2027 in a minute, and see how you are doing by day, month and year.

Get it on Etsy

One-time purchase. No subscription.

What it does

  • Today view with your habits and a check-in for each; start from templates such as drink water, read, walk or journal.
  • Habits can be planned daily, a number of times a week, or a number of times a month.
  • Habits with an amount goal, such as glasses of water or pages read.
  • Streaks counted in days, weeks or months, with best and current streak.
  • Comebacks are counted when you return after a miss, with a welcome-back note instead of a broken streak.
  • Mark a rest day so it does not count against you.
  • Month calendar with a day score, and a year heat map of every day.
  • Year table with the rate, best streak and comebacks for each habit.
  • Print your month or your year; archive habits and keep their check-ins.
  • Download calendar reminders (.ics) with a reminder time on each habit.

How it works

  • You buy it once on Etsy and download the file in a web browser (the Etsy phone app cannot download digital files).
  • On a computer, you open the app file in your browser. On a phone, you open the app's web address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and choose Add to Home Screen (Android: Install app).
  • Your data stays on your device. There is no account and no subscription.
  • After the first load it works without internet.
  • Languages: English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

What it does not do

  • The app cannot send notifications. Reminders are a calendar file that your phone's calendar app adds.
  • No cloud sync and no account. Each device keeps its own data; move it with a backup file.
  • It is limited to 200 habits.

Questions

Is this habit tracker a subscription?

No. You buy it once on Etsy. There is no monthly fee and no account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load it needs no internet. Your check-ins stay on your device.

Does the app send reminders?

It cannot send notifications itself. You can download a calendar file (.ics) and your phone's calendar will remind you.

What happens if I miss a day?

The app counts comebacks and shows a welcome-back note. You can also mark rest days.

Can I back up my habits?

Yes. Download a backup in the app and restore it on another device or after clearing browser data.
