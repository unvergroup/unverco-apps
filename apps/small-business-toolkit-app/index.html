Small Business Toolkit App: price it, quote it, pitch it, schedule it

Workbench puts four small business tools in one app. Makers and sellers can find a price that covers costs, fees and time. Tradespeople and freelancers can make quotes and invoices. Creators can build a rate card. Cafes, shops and small teams can plan a week of shifts.

Get it on Etsy

One-time purchase. No subscription.

What it does

  • Fair Price: enter costs, fees and your time to see the break-even price, a suggested price at your target margin, and a wholesale check.
  • Fair Price: warnings when your price loses money, then print a price list or export it as CSV.
  • Quote & Invoice: make a quote with line items and a deposit, convert it to an invoice, add payments and see the remaining balance.
  • Creator Rate Card: add your platforms, build a quote for each brand deal with usage and exclusivity fees, and see who has paid.
  • Shift Board: plan your team's shifts on a weekly board, use shift templates, and copy last week.
  • Shift Board warnings for overlapping shifts, weekly hour limits and short rest between shifts; these are reminders only.
  • Print schedules and a manager summary, or export shifts and deals as CSV.
  • Your business details print at the top of every document.

How it works

  • You buy it once on Etsy and download the file in a web browser (the Etsy phone app cannot download digital files).
  • On a computer, you open the app file in your browser. On a phone, you open the app's web address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and choose Add to Home Screen (Android: Install app).
  • Your data stays on your device. There is no account and no subscription.
  • After the first load it works without internet.
  • Languages: English, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese and Turkish.

What it does not do

  • No cloud sync and no team accounts. Each device keeps its own data; move it with a backup file. There is no password, so anyone using the same browser on the same device can see the data.
  • No automatic emails or messages, and it does not send documents for you.
  • Documents are not official tax invoices. Check your local invoicing rules.
  • Results are estimates, not tax, accounting or legal advice. Shift warnings are not advice on working-hours law.

Questions

What is in the Workbench small business toolkit?

Four tools: Fair Price, Quote & Invoice, Creator Rate Card and Shift Board, all in one app.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase on Etsy. There is no account and no monthly fee.

Does it work offline and where is my data?

Yes. After the first load it works without internet. Everything you enter stays in the browser on your device.

Which languages does it support?

English, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese and Turkish. The other nine are AI-assisted translations, so check the wording of printed documents before you send them.

Do I need all four tools?

No. Open only the ones you need. If you only want quotes and invoices, there is also a separate Quote & Invoice app.
