Quote and Invoice Maker App — deposits, payments, no subscription

Quote & Invoice is for freelancers, tradespeople and small businesses that want to send a clear quote, ask for a deposit, and then turn the accepted quote into an invoice. It tracks what has been paid and what is still due.

Get it on Etsy

One-time purchase. No subscription.

What it does

  • Create a quote with a client and line items, then save it.
  • Ask for a deposit as a percentage; totals show the remaining amount after the deposit.
  • Apply a discount, and set a tax rate on each line.
  • Convert an accepted quote to an invoice in one step.
  • Add payments to an invoice and see the remaining balance.
  • Statuses for quotes (Draft, Sent, Accepted, Declined, Expired) and invoices (Unpaid, Deposit paid, Paid, Overdue).
  • Filter by status and export your documents as CSV.
  • Print or save as PDF on Letter or A4, with your business details at the top.
  • Load sample data to try it, then delete it with one button.

How it works

  • You buy it once on Etsy and download the file in a web browser (the Etsy phone app cannot download digital files).
  • On a computer, you open the app file in your browser. On a phone, you open the app's web address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and choose Add to Home Screen (Android: Install app).
  • Your data stays on your device. There is no account and no subscription.
  • After the first load it works without internet.
  • Languages: English, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese and Turkish.

What it does not do

  • No cloud sync and no team accounts. Move data between devices with a backup file.
  • It does not send documents or emails for you; you send the printed or saved PDF yourself.
  • Documents are not official tax invoices. Check your local invoicing rules.
  • It is not a contract and does not check licensing, deposit limits or other rules for your trade.
  • No password: anyone using the same browser on the same device can see your data.

Questions

Is this quote and invoice maker a subscription?

No. You buy it once on Etsy. There is no monthly fee and no account.

Can I turn a quote into an invoice?

Yes. Open the quote and choose Convert to invoice, then add payments as they arrive.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load it needs no internet, and your clients and documents stay on your device.

Are the documents official tax invoices?

No. They are not official tax invoices. Check your local invoicing rules and ask your accountant.

How do I send a quote to a client?

Print it or save it as a PDF and send it yourself. The app does not send documents.
