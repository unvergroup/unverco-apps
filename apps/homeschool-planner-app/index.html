Homeschool Planner App — attendance, hours and grades, works offline

This homeschool planner helps you plan each child's lessons, log what was done, and keep a record of days, hours and grades. It keeps your own record; it does not know or check any state or country rules.

Get it on Etsy

One-time purchase. No subscription.

What it does

  • Set your school year with first and last day, school days of the week, and your own targets for days and hours.
  • Add up to 20 children, each with a color and grade, and subjects on set weekdays or in a loop that continues where you stopped.
  • Today view with each child's lessons: tap Done to log the lesson, or add an extra lesson.
  • Set a day as School day, Field trip, Sick or Break, and log group lessons or trips for several children at once.
  • Weekly plan per child, with a printable weekly checklist.
  • Progress against your targets: school days, total hours and core hours, plus hours by subject.
  • Attendance calendar per child and a grades list (percent or letter).
  • Print or save as PDF: attendance and hours log, subject summary, record summary, and a portfolio cover and year summary.
  • Reading list and activities library; export hours and attendance as CSV.

How it works

  • You buy it once on Etsy and download the file in a web browser (the Etsy phone app cannot download digital files).
  • On a computer, you open the app file in your browser. On a phone, you open the app's web address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and choose Add to Home Screen (Android: Install app).
  • Your data stays on your device. There is no account and no subscription.
  • After the first load it works without internet.
  • Languages: English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

What it does not do

  • It does not know, check or advise on homeschool laws, and does not file or submit anything.
  • It does not create official transcripts or calculate GPA.
  • No cloud sync and no account. Each device keeps its own data; move it with a backup file.
  • It does not include lesson content or curriculum.

Questions

Is the homeschool planner a subscription?

No. You buy it once on Etsy. There is no monthly fee and no account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load it needs no internet. Your records stay on your device.

Can I plan lessons for more than one child?

Yes. You can add up to 20 children, and give one subject to several children in one step.

Does it follow my state's homeschool rules?

No. It keeps a record of your days and hours against targets you set yourself. Check your own state's rules.

Can I print my records?

Yes. Print or save as PDF on Letter or A4, and export hours and attendance as CSV.
