Wedding Planner App: guest list, tables and place cards you can print

This wedding planner app is built for one job: getting your guest list from a spreadsheet to printed paper. Paste or import your list, seat your guests at tables, check for mistakes, and print a seating sign, place cards and a caterer sheet.

Get it on Etsy

One-time purchase. No subscription.

What it does

  • Paste a list or import a CSV file; the app guesses which column is which and you correct it. It reads up to 2,000 rows and does not add the same guests twice.
  • Mark guests as attending, pending or declined, and set meal options (rename them as you like).
  • Add tables with a number of seats each, and seat people with Move. Tick Move whole party to keep a family together.
  • Check before you print looks at eight things, such as guests without a table or tables that are over full. It never blocks printing.
  • Seating sign: choose a board size and a title and save as PDF. Declined guests are left off.
  • Place cards with a test sheet to line up your own card pack, with nudge settings in millimeters.
  • Caterer sheet in Letter or A4 with meal counts by table and by meal.
  • Mark as printed, then see Changes since printed to know which cards to reprint.
  • Try it first with 40 sample guests, then remove them with one button.

How it works

  • You buy it once on Etsy and download the file in a web browser (the Etsy phone app cannot download digital files).
  • On a computer, you open the app file in your browser. On a phone, you open the app's web address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and choose Add to Home Screen (Android: Install app).
  • Your data stays on your device. There is no account and no subscription.
  • After the first load it works without internet.
  • Languages: English, Spanish, French, German and Turkish.

What it does not do

  • No account, no cloud sync and no sharing between people or devices.
  • No invitations or RSVP forms, and no automatic messages.
  • It does not print for you: you print on your own printer or take the PDF to a print shop.
  • The caterer sheet does not list dietary or allergy notes; confirm those with each guest and your caterer.
  • Card layouts are calculated from measurements and not every card pack or printer has been tested, which is why the test sheet is there.

Questions

Is this wedding planner app a subscription?

No. You buy it once on Etsy. There is no monthly fee and no account.

Can I import my guest list from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Paste the cells or save the file as CSV and import it. Files ending in .xlsx cannot be opened directly, so save as CSV first.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the first load it works offline, and your list stays on your device.

Can I print place cards on my own card pack?

Yes. Print the test sheet on plain paper, line it up against a blank card sheet, and adjust with the nudge settings before printing your cards.

Does it send invitations?

No. It is a guest list, seating and printing tool. It has no RSVP forms and sends nothing.
