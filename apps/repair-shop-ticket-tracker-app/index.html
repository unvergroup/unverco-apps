Repair Shop Ticket Tracker App — check-in to pickup, works offline

Claimtag is a repair shop ticket tracker for small shops that fix phones, tablets, laptops and computers. It follows each device from check-in to pickup, so you always know what is waiting, what is in repair and what is ready.

Get it on Etsy

One-time purchase. No subscription.

What it does

  • Check-in: customer by phone number (earlier customers are suggested), device type, make and model, IMEI or serial number, and the problem in the customer's words.
  • Condition at check-in: case, screen, back, camera lens, buttons, ports, liquid contact, powers on, and accessories received.
  • Estimate, diagnosis fee and deposit received on each job; print the intake form in two copies, or a blank form.
  • Recognizes a device that was in your shop before and shows its earlier ticket.
  • Job board with New, Waiting, In repair and Ready for pickup columns, days in status, and a filter for jobs that need an update.
  • Ready-made customer messages (checked in, price approval, waiting for part, ready, reminder, review request) to copy or open in WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Find a job by name, phone, IMEI or serial number, or ticket number; filter by open, ready, closed or under warranty.
  • Pickup screen: tests at pickup, record payment, set warranty days, print a pickup slip or an invoice.
  • Export all jobs, or just the results you see, as CSV.

How it works

  • You buy it once on Etsy and download the file in a web browser (the Etsy phone app cannot download digital files).
  • On a computer, you open the app file in your browser. On a phone, you open the app's web address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and choose Add to Home Screen (Android: Install app).
  • Your data stays on your device. There is no account and no subscription.
  • After the first load it works without internet.
  • Languages: English, Spanish, French, German and Turkish.

What it does not do

  • No cloud sync between devices and no team accounts.
  • No automatic messages: WhatsApp and SMS open with ready text and you send it yourself.
  • It does not store passcodes, PINs or unlock patterns; the app warns you not to record them.
  • It is a record-keeping tool for your shop, not a payment processor.

Questions

Is Claimtag a subscription?

No. You buy it once on Etsy. There is no monthly fee and no account.

What kinds of repair shops is it for?

Small shops that repair phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers. A device can also be logged as Other.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load the app works without internet. WhatsApp and SMS messages need a connection on the device that sends them.

Can several people use it at once?

No. There are no team accounts or live sync. Data is stored on one device and you can move it with a backup file.

Can I print forms for customers?

Yes. You can print the intake form in two copies, a blank form, a pickup slip and an invoice.
