Custom web app for your small business

UNVERCO builds custom web apps for individuals and businesses. They run in the browser, install on a phone from the home screen, work offline and need no subscription. Your data stays on your device.

Who it is for

Small businesses, freelancers and teams with a job that spreadsheets and paper do badly: tracking jobs, pricing, bookings, orders or records. If you already use one of our ready-made apps and need something close to it, tell us what is different.

What you get

  • A web app made for your own workflow.
  • It installs on iPhone and Android from the browser and also runs on Windows and Mac.
  • It works offline and has no account or subscription.
  • Your data stays on your device, with a backup file you can save.

How it works

  • 1. Tell us what you need. Message the shop and describe the job, who uses it and what you handle today.
  • 2. We send a quote.
  • 3. We build it.
  • 4. We test it, including on iPhone and Android simulators.
  • 5. We deliver it.

What it does not include

  • No cloud sync between devices and no team accounts, unless you agree otherwise in your quote.
  • No promise of a price or a delivery time on this page; both are agreed in your quote.

Questions

Do I pay a monthly fee for a custom app?

The apps are built without a subscription and run in the browser on your device. Ask the shop about the terms of your project.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Apps are built to work offline after the first load, and they install on a phone from the browser.

Where is my data stored?

On your own device. The apps do not need an account or a server holding your data.
