Bakery Pricing Calculator App — recipe cost, orders and shopping list

Bakery Pricing & Orders helps a home baker answer three questions: what does each piece really cost, what should I charge, and which orders, deposits and ingredients do I need to deal with this week.

Get it on Etsy

One-time purchase. No subscription.

What it does

  • Pantry: enter each ingredient with package size, unit and the price you paid; units include g, kg, oz, lb, ml, l, cup, tbsp, tsp and pieces.
  • Recipes with ingredient lines, packaging, your time and an optional overhead percentage; the cost box updates as you type.
  • Cost per batch and per piece, and a suggested price from your target profit margin or markup.
  • A label shows whether your price Loses money, is Below target or On target.
  • When an ingredient price changes, every recipe that uses it is recalculated.
  • Orders with customer, pickup or delivery date, items, delivery fee, discount and deposit, moving from Inquiry to Confirmed, In progress, Delivered and Paid.
  • Print an order confirmation, a quote or an invoice.
  • Weekly plan: what to bake and a shopping list with packages to buy.
  • Monthly sales view with estimated cost and profit; export orders as CSV.

How it works

  • You buy it once on Etsy and download the file in a web browser (the Etsy phone app cannot download digital files).
  • On a computer, you open the app file in your browser. On a phone, you open the app's web address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and choose Add to Home Screen (Android: Install app).
  • Your data stays on your device. There is no account and no subscription.
  • After the first load it works without internet.
  • Languages: English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

What it does not do

  • No food safety, labeling, cottage food permit or tax advice.
  • It does not track stock on hand or expiry dates.
  • It does not take payments, send messages by itself or sync between devices.
  • Suggested prices use only your own costs and target; the app does not know local market prices.

Questions

Is this bakery pricing calculator a subscription?

No. You buy it once on Etsy. There is no monthly fee and no account.

How does it work out the cost of one piece?

It adds ingredients, packaging, your time at your hourly rate and overhead, then divides by the number of pieces in a batch.

What about cups and spoons?

For ingredients you buy by weight, you enter once what one cup of your own flour weighs. The app does not guess this number.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load it works without internet. Your recipes and orders stay on your device.

Can I track deposits and orders?

Yes. Each order shows the total, the suggested deposit, the deposit received and the balance.
